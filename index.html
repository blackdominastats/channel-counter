How to connect your channel

  1. Open YouTube Studio for the channel you want to connect.
  2. Go to Settings → Permissions.
  3. Choose the option to invite a user. If YouTube says your channel uses Brand Account permissions, follow YouTube's on-screen instructions for switching to channel permissions first.
  4. Invite nextcountsbot@gmail.com and choose Viewer (limited) if that role is offered for the invitation.
  5. Send the invitation and wait for NextCounts to process and publish the channel. This may take some time; check the NextCounts dashboard for its latest status.
  6. Return here and click Check connection. Once the channel appears in NextCounts' public Studio directory, this counter can use its unabbreviated count.
